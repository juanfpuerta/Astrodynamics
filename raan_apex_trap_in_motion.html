Why Gauss's Law Creates the Trap

Gauss Variational Equations:
di/dt = (r cos u / h) * f_h
dΩ/dt = (r sin u / (h sin i)) * f_h
At u = 90°, cos(90°) = 0 implies zero inclination change. But this holds strictly for infinitesimal continuous force. For a discrete impulse, the new orbit is forced through the peak of Orbit 1, which is not the apex of Orbit 2. The orbit continues to climb, causing an inclination overshoot!
